## Design and authority

- Keep plain PHP, PSR-4 namespaces, the checked-in PSR-12 gate and LF checkout policy. Use thin entry scripts and focused constructor dependencies. Add patterns for a demonstrated boundary, not file-count growth. No runtime model decisions or adaptive behavior without a demonstrated need.
- Separate typed application commands and queries with explicit handlers shared by CLI/HTTP. Queries must not construct providers or reconcile. The current table is the `GetLinkedPairTable` use case; HTTP authentication/representation belongs to `MonitoringController`. This is synchronous CQRS with one business SQLite store, not a queue, event log or separate read database.
- Reuse the explicitly injected business connection within its application context. Keep configuration, schema upgrades, transactions and canonical locking in persistence infrastructure. Do not add a process-global database Singleton or change saved plan JSON during structural refactors. Composer tooling stays outside the PHP runtime image. See [architecture](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md).
- Never rely on AI in compliance. Source content, models, dashboards, playback and skill instructions cannot decide policy satisfaction, select a conflict winner or supply approval. Document accountable policy owners and missing controls.
- Preserve title-only link opt-in and whole-plan review for every outbound state change. Approval binds pair, exact target/preimages/versions and policy version, checks fresh evidence and never comes from retry/source selection. Keep durable intent, unknown-outcome read-before-retry, no baseline advance until verified convergence and the canonical single-worker lock. Existing pending work must survive upgrades and receive the current gate.
- Required audit failures prevent subsequent effects. Best-effort telemetry cannot decide business correctness. The shared operator token grants broad authority; do not claim verified humans, individual attribution, agent scopes or separation of duties. The read-only business review skill's allowlist is guidance, not server-enforced privilege. [Governance](docs/GOVERNANCE.md) owns these limits.
- Preserve [tagged intake](docs/INTAKE.md): fresh open/tag/initial-description validation, marker and audited snapshot/attempt before POST, observed issue ID before linking, exact-marker recovery without blind resend, and unchanged legacy snapshots. Use the shared literal-text body formatter and fixed Zendesk link. Existing-body repairs need separate authorization. Bootstrap and queries remain provider-free.
- Preserve [comment delivery](docs/COMMENTS.md): immutable first-observed snapshots, audited attempt/version before append, unique internal read-back, no blind repeat after an attempt and a durable subsystem hold after public-note evidence. New notes wait for synced fields with no pending review and fresh canonical agreement. Comments never grant state approval.

Never reset accounts/volumes to satisfy bootstrap smoke. Keep cookies scoped by project; a cookie-name change needs a new login. Verify independent local sessions remain valid when changing that boundary. Monitoring-token rotation also recreates Grafana's startup-loaded reader.
